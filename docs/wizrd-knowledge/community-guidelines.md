> **Information notice only — this is not an approved community policy or a replacement for one.**

### Official policy source not found

The repository audit did not find an official MeToYou Community Guidelines or Code of Conduct document, and no approved official community policy was supplied for this document. Therefore, this document intentionally does **not** state behavioral rules, prohibited content, enforcement actions, appeal rights, or reporting response commitments.

UI report categories and moderation labels in the application are not substitutes for approved community guidelines. They must not be presented as the official policy.

### Instructions for WiZrd while this source is missing

- Say plainly that an approved Community Guidelines source is not available in the knowledge materials.
- Do not invent, infer, or paraphrase informal rules as official policy.
- Do not promise that a report will be reviewed, resolved by a deadline, or result in a particular enforcement action.
- Direct the user to an owner-approved support or policy location only after one is supplied and verified.

### Required owner input before adding official policy content

Provide the official approved policy document, its effective date and version, an authoritative publication location, and the approved description of reporting, review, enforcement, and appeals. Obtain any required legal review. Until an approved policy is supplied, this published document remains an information notice about the missing source, not a replacement policy.
